Question one
The Gap
If everything stayed exactly as it is today for another 90 days, what would be missing that makes your goal impossible?
Write without editing yourself. There are no wrong answers here.
Question two
The Bottleneck
Of everything you just named, which one — if it were solved tomorrow — would make the most other things easier or unnecessary?
There is usually one. It tends to be the thing you have been avoiding naming.
Question three
The Cliff
Which of the choices in front of you, if you made it today and it turned out to be wrong, would cost you something you cannot recover?
This is not about fear. It is about knowing which decisions are experiments and which ones are commitments.
Your commitment
The Direction
Name the direction you are choosing. Not the perfect answer. The one that is true right now.
You can change your mind. But naming it — even provisionally — is the work.
